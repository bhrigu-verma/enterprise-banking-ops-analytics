# Aegis Crest Financial - 90-Second Executive Demo Script

**Speaker Role:** Senior Operations Analytics Engineer / CADP Analyst Candidate  
**Target Audience:** Operations Directors, Business Analysts, J.P. Morgan Hiring Team  
**Goal:** Demonstrate business acumen, data-driven root cause analysis, and actionable financial recommendation in under 90 seconds.

---

## 🎬 0:00 - 0:15 | The Headline & Business Context
*(Visual: Displaying the Executive Scorecard tab on the interactive dashboard)*

> **"Hello! Today I'm walking through the Aegis Crest Financial Operations Analytics Platform — an enterprise analytics engine monitoring 860,000 transaction, lending, and customer service records across 5 geographic regions.**
> 
> **"In Q3 2024, our bank faced a serious operational dilemma: loan turnaround times rose 38% to nearly 4 days, customer complaint escalations surged 27%, and confirmed fraud write-offs hit $3.21M system-wide ($0.95M in FastTrack accounts). My objective was to pinpoint the root cause and deliver a defensible recovery strategy."**

---

## 🔍 0:15 - 0:45 | Root Cause Diagnosis & Data Evidence
*(Visual: Switching to Customer 360 & Onboarding tab, highlighting Region 2 Southeast and Region 5 Midwest West)*

> **"By aggregating our 13 database tables, I isolated the issue to two regions — Southeast Hub and Midwest West — following their rollout of the 'FastTrack' digital onboarding flow. 
> 
> To maximize digital growth, FastTrack bypassed manual credit checks for online applicants. As a result, average FICO scores in this cohort dropped from 705 to 644.6, while Debt-to-Income ratios expanded to 36.1%-45.0%. Underwriting queues became severely bottlenecked, while unverified accounts allowed Card Not Present fraud to surge."**

---

## ⚡ 0:45 - 1:15 | Interactive AI Differentiator & Query Execution
*(Visual: Clicking on the 'AI Natural-Language-to-SQL' tab, typing: 'Show regions with highest loan default write-offs', hitting Run Query)*

> **"To enable executives to explore this dataset instantly without writing code, I built a Natural-Language-to-SQL query engine. 
> 
> As you see here, when I ask for regional default write-offs or custom ratio questions, the engine dynamically generates valid SQL, executes it against SQLite in ~3 milliseconds with read-only safety validation, and confirms that Southeast Hub and Midwest West account for the majority of all bank default write-offs."**

---

## 🎯 1:15 - 1:30 | Strategic Recommendation & ROI
*(Visual: Showing the Process Improvement Diagram / Executive Memo report)*

> **"Rather than reverting digital onboarding completely, I recommended deploying a Conditional Risk-Based Review: automatically gating applications with FICO scores below 640 or loan amounts above $25,000 for secondary verification. 
> 
> Simulations prove this targeted rule recovers $3.96 million in annual default and fraud savings while preserving 82% of our digital onboarding speed. Thank you!"**
